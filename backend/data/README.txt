Coimbatore rural directory. Source: archived LGD reports dated 19 November 2026 at https://github.com/ramSeraph/opendata/releases/tag/lgd-latest (pri_local_bodies, pri_local_body_wards, gp_mapping, villages_by_blocks). Filter by unionCode then panchayatCode. Villages and wards are separate children of the selected panchayat. These files do not assign individual streets or revenue villages to wards. An empty village list indicates a missing mapping. Union is the intermediate elected local body; developmentBlocks records the development block names from the village mapping. Refresh before fieldwork if boundaries change.
